IOTA core

What it is

The core is the app itself: the chat window, the model runner, and the settings that tie every branch together.

How it works

Each branch plugs into the core. Adding a connector, a skill, or a knowledge base gives IOTA a new ability without changing how you use the app. You keep typing in the same chat, and IOTA decides which branch a request needs.

Editions

What it is

IOTA for Windows comes in two editions: PowerShell and EXE.

How it works

The PowerShell edition is driven by PowerShell for increased security and easier updates. It is strictly offline and uses RAG with local databases only. This is a security measure that protects the active model on the USB drive.

The EXE edition is faster, with enhanced animations. It can connect to cloud models and switch between local and cloud hosting with one click.

Local models

What it is

The AI model runs on your own PC, not on a remote server.

How it works

IOTA loads open-weight models from the USB kit and runs them on your graphics card or your processor. You can switch models for speed or quality, and a CPU Only mode works on PCs without a supported graphics card. No internet connection is needed to chat, and your conversations stay on your computer.

In the EXE edition, you can also switch to a cloud-hosted model with one click and switch back to local the same way. The PowerShell edition runs local models only.

MCP connectors

What it is

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets an AI app work with outside tools and data sources through small connector programs.

How it works

Each connector describes the actions it offers, such as reading a calendar, searching a drive, or querying a database. IOTA lists the connectors you have added, the model picks one when a request needs it, and IOTA shows you what it did. Connectors are optional, and you choose which ones to add.

Connectors that reach online services need the EXE edition. The PowerShell edition is strictly offline, so it does not reach online services.

Skills

What it is

Skills are focused add-ons for specific jobs. You start one with a slash command, or IOTA starts it when a request calls for it.

How it works

The skill does the task with tested code, for example drawing a chart or converting units exactly, and the model writes around the result. Charts, decks, and PDFs come out in the IOTA theme.

  • /chartBuild a chart from numbers or a table.
  • /deckMake a slide deck.

  • /ocrRead the text in an image or scan.
  • /transcribeTurn an audio recording into text.
  • /unitsConvert units exactly.
  • /finRun finance calculations.

Knowledge bases

What it is

A knowledge base is a set of your own files, such as notes, manuals, or reports, that IOTA can search when it answers.

How it works

Add files to a knowledge base and IOTA indexes them on your PC. When you ask a question, IOTA searches all of your knowledge bases by meaning, pulls in the most relevant passages, and answers from them. Nothing is uploaded.

IOTA Orchestrator

Knowledge bases integrate with the IOTA Orchestrator, allowing local data parsing at speeds over 30 Gb/s when run on a workstation laptop with an NVIDIA RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell GPU.

RAG pipeline

What it is

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) means the model looks up relevant passages in your files before it answers, instead of relying only on what it learned in training.

How it works

  1. Ingest.Files are read and converted to plain text.
  2. Chunk.The text is split into short passages, each about one idea.
  3. Embed.Each passage becomes a list of numbers (an embedding) from the bge-base model, so passages with similar meaning sit close together.
  4. Retrieve.Your question is embedded the same way and the closest passages are found. Retrieval controls set how many passages are used and how close a match must be.
  5. Ground.The chosen passages go into the model's context with instructions to answer from them.
  6. Answer.The model writes a reply based on those passages, which keeps it tied to your sources.

Memory

What it is

Memory holds notes and facts you want IOTA to use in later chats, such as your role, your projects, or your preferences.

How it works

Memory is a local collection of notes. You can add a useful chat to it, and IOTA retrieves from it in later conversations the same way it searches a knowledge base. It is stored only on your PC, and you can edit or delete it at any time.

Chats and projects

What it is

The chat sidebar keeps your conversations organized into projects.

How it works

Each chat is saved on your PC with its date and time. Group chats into projects, reopen any past conversation, and see how many chats each project holds.

Personalities

What it is

Personalities change how IOTA writes and lays out an answer.

How it works

Pick one to suit the task, such as a direct default, a researcher that gives findings and a confidence level, or a manager that lists action items. The facts stay the same; only the style and structure change.

06 / IOTA RAG

Retrieval-augmented generation for your documents and data

IOTA RAG is our proprietary, expanded form of retrieval-augmented generation. We set it up on your documents and data and support it in use. It finds the right passages before the model answers, so a smaller model can give grounded answers with less computing power.

Select a stage to see what it does.

  1. Ingest. Files such as documents, notes, and reports are read and converted to plain text.

  2. Chunk. The text is split into short passages, each about one idea, so each can be found on its own.

  3. Embed. Each passage becomes a list of numbers called an embedding. Passages with similar meaning get similar numbers.

  4. Retrieve. Your question is turned into numbers the same way, and the passages closest in meaning are found.

  5. Ground. The chosen passages are given to the model with instructions to answer from them.

  6. Answer. The model writes its reply from those passages, which keeps the answer tied to your sources.

IOTA expanded. IOTA RAG is our proprietary, expanded form of RAG. It searches all of your knowledge banks at once, lets you tune how many passages are used and how close a match must be, and runs on a single PC, your own servers, or the cloud.

07 / Investment Management

Sector allocation strategies

IOTA Investment Management LLC develops private investment strategies centered on sector allocation. Our algorithms evaluate market sectors and set how a portfolio is weighted across them.

  • Sector-level view. The strategies work at the level of market sectors and decide how much weight each sector receives.
  • Rules-based process. Allocation decisions come from defined algorithms, so the same inputs lead to the same decisions.
  • Ongoing review. Models, data sources, and rules are reviewed and updated as markets and data change.
