Iota - Word Box Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Published by Iota LLC.

The short version

Iota - Word Box is an offline word puzzle game. There are no accounts and no sign in. Your scores, streaks and saved games are kept only on your phone. Iota LLC does not run any servers that receive your game data.

The game can show ads through Google AdMob and sell one optional purchase through Google Play. Those services are run by Google and are explained below.

The daily puzzle

To get the daily puzzle, the app downloads a small public file from this website (iotallc.github.io), which is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives a normal web request, which includes your IP address. No game data is sent. GitHub handles that request under its own privacy policy (GitHub Privacy Statement). If your phone is offline, the app uses a built-in daily word instead.

What stays on your phone

  • Your game. Your Stars, each mode's score and streak, games in progress, and your settings (such as music and sound).
  • Purchase status. If you buy ad-free play, the app keeps a note of the purchase so it can remember it.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage and is never sent to Iota LLC. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this data in your Google account backup, which Google handles under the Google Privacy Policy. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it from your phone.

No account, no tracking by us

There is no account, no sign in, and no profile. Iota LLC does not use analytics or tracking tools in Iota - Word Box, and we do not sell your data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad after every third finished game, and you can choose to watch a short rewarded ad for a hint. Ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares data with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location,
  • your Android advertising ID and app set ID,
  • ad interactions such as app launches, taps and video views,
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance.

This data goes to Google, not to Iota LLC. Google uses it under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your phone's Android settings under Privacy, Ads. Where the law requires it, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Ad-free play is a one-time $1.99 purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment. Iota LLC never sees your card or payment details. The app receives only a confirmation of the purchase from Google Play and keeps it on your phone so the purchase works after the app restarts. Google handles purchase data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Iota - Word Box is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Buy ad-free play in Settings to stop ads.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Because Iota LLC does not receive your game data, there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side, except emails you chose to send us, which we delete on request. For data held by Google or GitHub, see their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
